Meet Pepe

Pepe is a brave adventurer from a small Mexican village. One day, the evil chicken boss "El Pollo Loco" stole all the precious bottles from his town. Now Pepe must travel through dangerous lands to defeat El Pollo Loco and bring back peace to his village!
